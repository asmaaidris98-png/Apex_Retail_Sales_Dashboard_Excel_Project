# 📊 Apex Retail Sales Dashboard | Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Apex Retail Sales Dashboard** is an interactive sales analytics dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze retail sales performance across different regions, product categories, and payment methods.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights, allowing users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, and compare regional performance.

Designed with an interactive layout and dynamic filtering capabilities, this dashboard provides a centralized view of business performance to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales and profitability.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Identify top-performing salespeople.
- Track sales trends over time.
- Evaluate product category profitability.
- Understand customer payment preferences through quantity sold.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides an overview of three essential business metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| 💰 Total Sales | Overall revenue generated |
| 📈 Total Profit | Total profit earned |
| 📦 Total Quantity Sold | Total units sold |

### 2. Sales by Region
A column chart comparing sales performance across four geographical regions:
- East
- North
- South
- West

This visualization helps identify high-performing regions and regional sales differences.

### 3. Top 5 Salespersons
A horizontal bar chart highlighting the top five salespeople based on sales performance.

This allows for quick comparisons of individual contributions to overall revenue.

### 4. Profit by Category
A pie chart illustrating the distribution of profit across six product categories:
- Clothing
- Electronics
- Furniture
- Home Decor
- Others
- Sports

This provides insight into which product categories contribute most to overall profitability.

### 5. Sales Trend
A line chart displaying sales performance over time.

This visualization helps identify fluctuations, patterns, and changes in sales activity throughout the reporting period.

### 6. Quantity by Payment Mode
A column chart comparing the quantity of products sold through different payment methods:
- Card
- Cash
- Online

This helps identify customer payment preferences and purchasing behavior.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Filters

The dashboard includes interactive Excel slicers that allow users to filter the data dynamically.

Available filters include:

- **Region:** East, North, South, West
- **Payment Mode:** Card, Cash, Online
- **Category:** Clothing, Electronics, Furniture, Home Decor, Others, Sports

Users can select individual filters or combine multiple selections to explore specific segments of the business.

All connected KPIs and visualizations update based on the selected filters, allowing for a more focused analysis.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the Excel workbook from this repository.
2. Open the workbook in Microsoft Excel.
3. Navigate to the dashboard worksheet.
4. Use the slicers on the left side to filter by region, payment mode, or category.
5. Observe how the KPIs and charts dynamically update based on your selections.
6. Clear the filters to return to the overall sales overview.
